THE RESEARCH

From stem patterns to predictions

A local application for exploring dragon fruit stem disease classification.

What the CNN does

MobileNetV3 Small, EfficientNet-B0, and ResNet18 are convolutional neural networks. They learn patterns such as texture, colour, and lesion shapes from labelled training images.

This app loads your trained checkpoint, prepares the uploaded image using the evaluation resize, crop, and normalization, and displays its predicted class and softmax scores.

Your workflow

  1. Select the model and experiment.
  2. Upload a clear photograph of a stem.
  3. Analyze the image and inspect all class scores.
  4. Explore the saved test metrics in Model Comparison.

Understand the datasets

A — MultiOrgan, stem only: Healthy, Rust, Scab, Stem Canker, and Sunburn.

B — Common stem disease: Anthracnose, Cactus Virus X, Healthy, and Stem Canker.

C — Pitahaya stem disease: Anthracnose, Brown Stem Spot, Gray Blight, Healthy, Soft Rot, and Stem Canker.

Multiclass experiments use each dataset's own training, validation, and test splits. Binary experiments train on A, B, or A + B and evaluate the shared Healthy / Stem Canker classes in C.

Research scope and privacy

Uploaded images are processed in memory on this laptop and are not saved by the app. Inference runs on the CPU and does not require your friend's GPU or an internet connection after setup.

The app cannot detect every disease, reject all unrelated images, or provide a confirmed diagnosis. Scores are uncalibrated model outputs. Dataset independence, label consistency, related-image leakage, and performance across repeated runs still require research review.
